# DTDC document reference pack

Reviewed from the four supplied PDFs on 5 November 2026. These files are DTDC channel-partner/customer templates and reference material. They are source material for AN Logistics configuration; they are not automatically accepted legal terms or confirmed account credentials.

## Source documents

- Letterhead / channel-partner print file: identifies an authorised DTDC channel partner, DTDC Express Limited, DTDC House, Bengaluru, and a partner business address.
- Sales Agreement: contains customer onboarding, courier/customer information, contract period, credit approval, products and services, rate tables, risk coverage, surcharges, billing/payment SOP, pickup SOP, credit-sale terms, GST declaration and non-GST declaration.
- Sales Quotation: contains document and parcel rate schedules for Standard, Priority, Assured Delivery, Ground Express and Ground Cargo, plus fuel, GST, COD/to-pay, insurance, packaging, risk, special-destination and volumetric-weight terms.
- Volumetric Weight notice: Air divisor 5000; surface divisor 4750.

## Facts carried into the application

- GST must be represented as a separate tax component; the quotation template states 18% GST as its example.
- Volumetric weight must use the greater of actual and volumetric weight.
- The supplied documents use both 5000 (air) and 4750 (surface); the applicable divisor must be tied to the selected service, not hardcoded across all DTDC services.
- RTO is described as charged at forwarding rates.
- The quotation template includes fuel, COD/to-pay, insurance, packaging, ODA, special-destination and other surcharge concepts.
- Declared-value risk options and restricted/prohibited-goods declarations are part of onboarding and booking.
- The agreement template includes billing cycle, credit terms, credit limit, payment collection, pickup schedule, claims and retention fields.
